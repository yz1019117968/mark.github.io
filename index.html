Short Bio

I'm currently a tenure-track Assistant Professor at the School of Computer Science and Technology, Shandong University (SDU). In Oct. 2023, I received my Ph.D. degree in Computer Science from the City University of Hong Kong (CityU), under the supervision of Prof. Jacky Wai Keung. From Jan. to Sept. 2022, I was very honored to visit the Key Laboratory of High Confidence Software Technologies, Ministry of Education (Peking University), where my supervisors are Prof. Zhi Jin and Prof. Ge Li. My current research focuses on software engineering, automated code understanding and generation. I have published over 20 journal and conference research papers in top venues, such as FSE, ASE, ISSTA (山东大学首篇), TSE, TOSEM, EmSE, ICPC, SANER. Before pursuing the Ph.D., I received my B.Mgt. degree in Information System from the University of Jinan (UJN) in 2018 and received my M.Sc. degree in Computer Science with Distinction from the CityU in 2019.

I'm actively looking for self-motivated CS students (local/international) with proficient programming capability and experience in deep learning/PLM/LLM (e.g., Python), full-stack website/mobile App development (e.g., Java, JavaScript, and Node.js), or system programming (e.g., C/C++ and Rust). I have one quota for the master's program per year (2026Fall: 0/1), and our group also welcomes undergraduate students to participate in our research activities and group meetings. Feel free to drop me your CV. Thanks!
